Help

How NetSentinel works

Scanner knocks on devices you choose and reports which ports answer. Live traffic listens to the packets on the network the server is connected to and flags suspicious patterns. Alerts explain each finding in plain English.

NetSentinel is self-hosted: everything runs on your own server and nothing is sent anywhere else.

Who can do what

Please scan responsibly

Only scan and monitor networks you own or have written permission to test. Unauthorised scanning can be illegal (for example under the US Computer Fraud and Abuse Act and similar laws elsewhere). Every scan is recorded in the audit log.

Words you'll see

Live capture requirements

Packets are captured by the server, so it sees the traffic of the network it's plugged into (a switch mirror port is needed to see other devices' traffic). The server process needs root or the NET_RAW capability (Windows: Administrator and Npcap). Scanning and the demo need neither.

Try the demo

Pretend traffic is played through the same detection engine as real traffic. Demo items are labelled and never touch your network or your SIEM log file.
